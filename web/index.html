Kishi plays chess and shogi with engines written in Rust and compiled to WebAssembly. It thinks on this device, in Web Workers, and works offline. Its evaluation network is learning by self-play; until it beats the hand-written evaluation, Kishi plays with that.

Chess pieces: Chessnut by Alexis Luengas (Apache 2.0). Shogi pieces are drawn by Kishi in your device's Japanese typeface. Type: Geist (OFL). Source on GitHub
