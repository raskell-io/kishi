Kishi is a chess engine written in Rust and compiled to WebAssembly. It runs on this device, in Web Workers, and works offline. Its evaluation network is learning by self-play; until it beats the hand-written evaluation, Kishi plays with that.

Pieces by Colin M.L. Burnett (GPLv2+). Type: Geist (OFL). Source: raskell-io/kishi.
